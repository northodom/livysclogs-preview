17:T1936,<p>Dansko makes a wide range of clogs and mules. For kitchen and restaurant work, this guide covers two closed-back models: the Professional and the XP 2.0. Both are sized the same way. They differ most in the sole, the footbed, and whether Dansko makes a slip-resistance claim. Our River North kitchen guide picks the Professional in black patent for chefs and line cooks; this guide sets out what Dansko says about both, so you can check either one against your kitchen&#39;s footwear rule.</p>
<p><em>Livy&#39;s Clogs isn&#39;t a medical provider. If you have foot pain, talk with a podiatrist.</em></p>
<h2 id="the-two-models">The two models</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dansko Professional (black patent, women&#39;s sizing):</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003VVTU38?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check price on Amazon</a></li>
<li><strong>Dansko XP 2.0:</strong> described below from Dansko&#39;s own page. We don&#39;t link a listing for it here yet.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="first-how-dansko-sizing-works">First, how Dansko sizing works</h2>
<p>Dansko says its shoes are sized &quot;according to European (EU) whole size standards&quot; (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/faqs" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko FAQ</a>), so each pair of US half sizes maps to one EU size. On its chart, a women&#39;s US 7½ and 8 are both a 38, and a women&#39;s US 8½ and 9 are both a 39 (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/size-charts" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko size chart</a>). For closed-back clogs, Dansko says:</p>
<ul>
<li>the fit may feel slightly snug when you step in</li>
<li>your toes should not touch the front</li>
<li>your heel should move up and down freely as you walk</li>
</ul>
<p>Dansko&#39;s FAQ adds that the correct size leaves a pinky&#39;s width of space between your heel and the back lining of the clog (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/faqs" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko FAQ</a>). Most styles are made in medium width, with narrow and wide in noted styles (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/size-charts" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko size chart</a>). Our <a href="/livysclogs-preview/dansko-professional-review/">Dansko Professional fit guide</a> goes further into fit.</p>
<h2 id="dansko-professional">Dansko Professional</h2>
<p>Dansko&#39;s product page lists a roomy reinforced toe box, a contoured footbed with built-in natural arch support, a padded instep collar, a heel counter that lets the heel move while giving lateral stability, an inner frame, and an anti-fatigue PU rocker bottom with a wide heel strike. It lists the Professional as APMA Accepted, with a 2-inch heel and a 0.8-inch platform. The black patent version linked here is described as &quot;patent leather that&#39;s easy to wipe clean,&quot; and that page offers regular width only (<a href="https://dansko.com/products/professional-black-patent" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko</a>). In its clog guide, Dansko says the Professional has &quot;a firmer footbed and a more structured feel&quot; (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/clog-guide" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko clog guide</a>).</p>
<p>Dansko makes no slip-resistance claim for the Professional on its product page. If your workplace requires slip-resistant shoes, check the listing against that rule before you buy. The Amazon listing linked here is women&#39;s sizing; check your size and color on the listing.</p>
<p><strong>Consider it if:</strong> you want a firm, structured closed-back clog with a wipe-clean patent upper, for kitchen or restaurant work where the footwear rule doesn&#39;t call for a maker&#39;s slip claim. Our <a href="/livysclogs-preview/work-clogs/chicago/river-north/upscale-restaurant-kitchens/chef-line-cook/">River North kitchen guide</a> picks it for both the chef and the line cook.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003VVTU38?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check price on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h2 id="dansko-xp-2-0">Dansko XP 2.0</h2>
<p>The details here come from Dansko&#39;s product page for the XP 2.0 in Black Waterproof Pull Up (<a href="https://dansko.com/products/xp-2-0-black-waterproof-pull-up" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko XP 2.0</a>). We don&#39;t link an XP 2.0 listing from this guide yet. If you shop for one, check that the listing&#39;s colorway and width match what you want, because Dansko&#39;s details vary by colorway.</p>
<p>On that page, Dansko says the XP 2.0 has a &quot;patented slip-resistant rubber outsole suitable for dry, wet, and oily/wet surfaces.&quot; It doesn&#39;t name a slip-test standard, so if your employer requires one, confirm with Dansko or your employer before you buy.</p>
<p>Dansko lists a removable footbed of dual-density PU with Dansko Natural Arch technology and memory foam, over a lightweight EVA midsole, and says it accommodates most standard and custom orthotics. It also lists a padded instep collar, a roomy reinforced toe box, leather socklining, and patented stapled construction, with a 1.7-inch heel and a 0.98-inch platform. It shows the APMA Accepted badge for this colorway. That page offers Regular and Wide widths, and Dansko describes the leather as waterproof only for select styles. For the Black Waterproof Pull Up, Dansko says to wipe the clog clean with a damp cloth and maintain it with Dansko Leather Cream. Its clog guide says the XP 2.0 is lighter than its Professional clog (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/clog-guide" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko clog guide</a>).</p>
<p><strong>Consider it if:</strong> you want a removable footbed that Dansko says takes most orthotics, or your workplace&#39;s footwear rule calls for a maker-stated slip-resistant outsole.</p>
<h2 id="which-one">Which one?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Firm and structured, with a wipe-clean patent upper:</strong> Professional (black patent). Dansko makes no slip claim for it, so check the listing against your workplace&#39;s footwear rule.</li>
<li><strong>Removable footbed for orthotics, or a maker-stated slip-resistant outsole:</strong> XP 2.0.</li>
</ul>
