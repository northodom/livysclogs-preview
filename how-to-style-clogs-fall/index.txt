1b:T105c,<p>Clogs are the fall 2026 shoe that goes with almost everything. They sit right between summer sandals and winter boots, and you can wear them bare-ankled on mild days and add socks as it gets colder (<a href="https://www.clairemac.co.uk/2026/09/how-to-style-clogs-in-autumn/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Claire Mac</a>). DSW says they look especially good with straight-leg or wide-leg denim (<a href="https://www.dsw.com/articles/inspiration/fall-shoe-trends" rel="noopener" target="_blank">DSW</a>). Here are eight outfit formulas to try.</p>
<h2 id="1-wide-leg-jeans-cozy-knit-suede-clogs">1. Wide-leg jeans + cozy knit + suede clogs</h2>
<p>The easiest fall outfit there is. Let the hem sit just over the top of the clog. Try it with <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000GLNXCA?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Birkenstock Boston Soft Footbed</a> or the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CKWS9W6Q?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">CUSHIONAIRE Hobby</a>.</p>
<h2 id="2-straight-jeans-chunky-socks">2. Straight jeans + chunky socks</h2>
<p>Cream or rust ribbed socks with brown clogs is <em>the</em> fall look. For extra warmth, go with a lined pair like the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CQTKWYTY?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Birkenstock Boston Shearling</a>.</p>
<h2 id="3-midi-or-maxi-dress-clogs">3. Midi or maxi dress + clogs</h2>
<p>Stylists pair chunky clogs with flowy dresses for contrast. Sandra Bullock recently wore studded clogs with a maxi gown and a blazer (<a href="https://www.usmagazine.com/stylish/news/sandra-bullock-70s-shoe-style-look-for-less/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Us Weekly</a>).</p>
<h2 id="4-all-one-color">4. All one color</h2>
<p>Byrdie notes that a mostly one-color outfit makes chunky clogs look sleek and high-fashion (<a href="https://www.byrdie.com/clogs-fashion-trend-11956525" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Byrdie</a>). Try chocolate on chocolate, or all cream with tan clogs.</p>
<h2 id="5-tailored-trousers-clogs-for-the-office">5. Tailored trousers + clogs for the office</h2>
<p>A relaxed trouser and a crisp shirt with a leather clog keeps it polished. For long workdays, see our <a href="/livysclogs-preview/best-clogs-for-nurses/">work clog guide</a>.</p>
<h2 id="6-leggings-oversized-sweater-lined-clogs">6. Leggings + oversized sweater + lined clogs</h2>
<p>Weekend errands, sorted. The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09P1HTTSJ?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Classic Lined Clog</a> is the cozy, easy option.</p>
<h2 id="7-platform-clogs-mini-skirt-tights">7. Platform clogs + mini skirt + tights</h2>
<p>Platforms add height and a little &#39;70s attitude. Try the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B088ZM2YRC?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Classic Platform</a>.</p>
<h2 id="8-add-charms-or-pops-of-color">8. Add charms or pops of color</h2>
<p>Customization is a big part of 2026&#39;s clog trend (<a href="https://www.byrdie.com/clogs-fashion-trend-11956525" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Byrdie</a>). Add a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09PC5YFD2?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Jibbitz 5-pack</a> of charms to your Crocs, or pick an animal-print or burgundy accent (both are fall 2026 trends per <a href="https://www.dsw.com/articles/inspiration/fall-shoe-trends" rel="noopener" target="_blank">DSW</a>).</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="fall-2026-color-cheat-sheet">Fall 2026 color cheat sheet</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Cognac, caramel, chestnut, chocolate:</strong> go with cream, denim, olive, and navy.</li>
<li><strong>Burgundy:</strong> goes with gray, black, camel, and denim.</li>
</ul>
